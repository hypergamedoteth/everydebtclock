This website, everydebtclock.com, is operated by Hypergame LLC, a Texas limited liability company ("we", "us"). This page explains what happens to information about you when you visit. There are no accounts and no sign-ups, and we don't ask for your name or email to use the site.

What we collect

The site has a search box ("Find a clock") on the home page and the /states/ and /countries/ pages, and a place picker on the home page. What you type in the search box stays in your browser: it filters a list of places (a static file, /api/search.json) that your browser downloads, and the text is not sent to us or anyone else. We don't collect your name, email or anything else you type. The only information we or our services receive is the technical information your browser sends whenever it loads a page, handled by the services below.

Analytics: GoatCounter

The site's pages include a script from GoatCounter, a privacy-focused analytics service. It does not use cookies and does not track you across other websites. It records the page you viewed, the site that referred you, your browser and operating system type, screen size, your browser's language setting, and an approximate country. Your IP address is used only to work out the country and to avoid counting the same visit twice; GoatCounter does not store it. We see only totals, such as how many people viewed a page today. See GoatCounter's privacy policy.

Hosting and security: GitHub Pages and Cloudflare

The site's files are hosted on GitHub Pages and delivered through Cloudflare, which provides the HTTPS connection and protects the site from attacks. Like any web server, they process your IP address and browser details to deliver pages, and may keep these in logs for security and troubleshooting. If Cloudflare needs to check for automated traffic, it may set a strictly necessary security cookie (such as __cf_bm or cf_clearance); these are not used for advertising.

Analytics: Cloudflare Web Analytics

Because the site is delivered through Cloudflare, Cloudflare Web Analytics may also be on. It can be enabled at Cloudflare's network without a script in our pages. It is privacy-focused, does not use cookies and does not track you across other websites. It counts page views, referrers, browser and device type, an approximate country, and page-load performance. We see only totals. See Cloudflare's privacy policy.

Cookies and local storage

The site does not set its own first-party cookies today. It uses your browser's local storage in three places, all only on your device:

  • When you pick a place in the home page's place picker, the site saves that choice (a place name such as "texas", under the key edc.place) so the same place can show next time.
  • When you dismiss the cookie and ads notice, the site saves that choice under the key edc.notice so the notice stays hidden.
  • When the add-to-home-screen prompt is shown, the site saves that under the key edc.a2hs so the prompt is not shown again. Nothing about that choice is sent to us.

If the page registers its service worker, the browser keeps a copy of the stylesheet, scripts, and icons so a home-screen icon can open. Debt figures are requested again each visit and are not stored in that cache. The service worker does not set a cookie and does not record your visit.

You can remove both by clearing this site's data in your browser. The live counters are calculated in your browser from data files on our own site. The site does not look up your location: the featured place is a fixed default until you choose another.

Advertising (if and when ads are shown)

We may show ads, for example through Google AdSense. If and when ads are shown, these rules apply:

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising at youradchoices.com.

A short notice about cookies and ads appears at the bottom of site pages (not on the live ticker, and not on the embeddable clock widgets). We will keep this policy current if ads go live or if the vendors change.

Data downloads

The site publishes free CSV files and charts of official public figures (and projections labelled as such): US debt by year, by president, per person, interest, state debt, and country debt. These files do not include information about visitors. Anyone may download them. They are public data, not personal data.

Links and sharing

The site links to official sources (such as the US Treasury, Census Bureau, IRS, Federal Reserve, BEA and the IMF) and offers a "share on X" link. Those sites have their own privacy policies; nothing is sent to them unless you click through. Other websites may embed our counter widget; when they do, your browser loads it from our site under this same policy. The embeddable clock widgets themselves set no cookies and load no analytics.

Children

The site is a general-audience information site. It is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from anyone.
