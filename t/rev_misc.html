How it's calculated. MODELED ESTIMATE, not an official published figure. Treasury does not publish miscellaneous receipts (incl. fed earnings) day by day, so we take 0.88% of federal revenue (its share of the monthly Treasury statements over trailing 12 months of MTS (2025-11 to 2026-10)) and apply it to the daily re-based FY2027 total. Real monthly figures can differ a lot from this, especially early in the fiscal year and in months where payments shift by a weekend. The official figure is the MTS Table 9 line when Treasury publishes the month. FY2026 final (MTS): $46.7B.

Updated Nov 19, 2026

Source. Treasury MTS Table 9 (share) × daily total: modeled estimate, daily total as of Nov 19, 2026.

* Per person (projected population) divides by the US population projected to now: the official Census Vintage 2025 estimate for July 1, 2025 (341,784,857), projected forward at Census's stated growth rate (342,779,786 at build time). Per tax return filed divides by 162,754,810 individual income tax returns filed in FY2025 (IRS Data Book, Table 1-2); a joint return counts once and returns with zero tax liability are included.
