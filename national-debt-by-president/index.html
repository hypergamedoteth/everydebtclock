    Questions

    Is this the debt on inauguration day?

    Where Debt to the Penny has a record, yes: the last close on or before the date. Before Apr 1, 1993, the table uses Treasury's annual Historical Debt Outstanding record on or before the date, which is not inauguration day.

    Does the change show what a president added to the debt?

    No. It is the difference between the Treasury total at the start and at the end. Term lengths differ, and spending and tax laws are passed by Congress.

    How does the table treat a term that started after a death or resignation?

    That term is listed under the person who took office: Tyler, Fillmore, Andrew Johnson, Arthur, Theodore Roosevelt, Coolidge, Truman, Lyndon Johnson, and Ford. The amounts are still the Treasury records on those dates.

    What is the national debt in the Donald Trump term that started Jan 20, 2025?

    That term is still in progress. Debt at the start was $36,206,593,315,575 as of Jan 17, 2025 (Debt to the Penny, the last close on or before the date). The latest Debt to the Penny close is $40,709,780,700,317.02 as of Nov 19, 2026. The change between those two Treasury figures is $4,503,187,384,742.

    Where do the debt-by-president figures come from?

    From Apr 1, 1993 on, each amount is the last Debt to the Penny close on or before the date. Earlier amounts are the last Historical Debt Outstanding record on or before the date. The current term ends at the latest close, $40,709,780,700,317.02 as of Nov 19, 2026.
