Each figure is total debt outstanding of the California state government and of local governments (counties, cities, school districts and special districts), as the Census Bureau defines it. It is the same total as the California debt clock. It is not the state's own annual report, and it does not include unfunded pensions or a share of the federal debt. Census dates each survey year June 30. Amounts are published in thousands of dollars, so the state and local parts can miss the total by a thousand or two.

Survey years 2017–2024 are Census's current time-series table, the same figures as the debt clock. Earlier years are the summary table Census published for that survey year.

Not in this table: 2001, 2003. Census published a national summary for those survey years, not the by-state workbook used for the other years, so they are left out rather than estimated. A change that crosses a missing year says how many years it covers.

The live clock is the California debt clock. The same kind of table for the United States is US national debt by year. Machine-readable copy: debt_by_year.json.
