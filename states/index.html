    US State Debt Clocks

    Every place with a debt clock, built from figures its own government publishes. A clock ticks only where the official trend is reliable; otherwise the official figure is shown as published. Each clock shows who publishes the numbers and how often, with every number linked to its source. Most of these governments report their debt once a year or once a month, so the ticking numbers are labelled projections from the latest official figures. Each tile says what it counts: state clocks count state and local government debt (cities, counties, school and special districts included), and countries are shown in their own currency. The World Debt Clock adds one stock per economy in US dollars, using the best source available.
