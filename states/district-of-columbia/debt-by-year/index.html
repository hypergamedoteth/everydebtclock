Each figure is total debt outstanding of the District of Columbia government, as the Census Bureau defines it. Census has no separate state-government level for the District, so the total is the local-government line. It is the same total as the District of Columbia debt clock. It does not include a share of the federal debt. Census dates each survey year June 30. Amounts are published in thousands of dollars.

Survey years 2017–2024 are Census's current time-series table, the same figures as the debt clock. Earlier years are the summary table Census published for that survey year.
