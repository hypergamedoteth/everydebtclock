How it works

Official annual figures · projection labelled · sources linked

The live number = latest official total + (last year's official change per second × seconds since that total). The Census Bureau publishes the District's debt once a year, not daily, so this is a projection between annual reports, not an official figure.

projected = 22,904,118,000 + 30.5602 × (seconds since Jun 30, 2024 11:59:59 PM CT)

The rate is the official change from Jun 30, 2023 to Jun 30, 2024: $22,904,118,000 − $21,937,732,000 = $966,386,000 over 366 days = $30.56 per second. The actual path is lumpy (bonds are sold in batches and repaid on schedules), so the projection will drift; when new annual reports come out, the clock re-bases to the new official figures.

How often it's published. Annual. The Census Bureau's Annual Survey of State and Local Government Finances comes out about two years after the fiscal year (survey year 2024: July 2026; survey year 2023: July 2025). The next Census figures, for survey year 2025, are expected around mid-2027, based on the last two release dates.

Per resident divides by the Census Bureau's estimate of 693,645 residents on Jul 1, 2025. It is not projected forward, so the live per-resident figure rises only with the debt.

Note. Census publishes the District's debt as $22.90 billion for 2024 (coefficient of variation 0.00%) and $21.94 billion for 2023. Census has no separate state government for the District, so the whole total is local debt.

Note. Census's figures are the newest official totals that use one definition for every state and the District, but they lag about two years, so the live clock projects from June 30, 2024. The District's own audited annual report is fresher but is not used here, so the District stays comparable with the states.

Note. The 2023→2024 change (+4.4%) is large enough to measure and small enough to extend, so the live number continues it as a straight line from June 30, 2024. Treat it as a projection, not a reported figure.

Note. Not included: pension and retiree-health liabilities, and the District's share of the federal debt.

Note. The District's fiscal year ends September 30, so Census's survey year 2024 holds the District government's fiscal year that ended September 30, 2023. Census survey year 2024 covers each government's fiscal year that ended between July 1, 2023 and June 30, 2024; the clock dates it June 30, 2024, the end of that window.

Note. Figures from source tables may not add exactly because of rounding in the source.
