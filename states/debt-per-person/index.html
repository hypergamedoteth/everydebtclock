Questions

Which state has the highest debt per person?

The highest state and local debt per person is District of Columbia at $33,020. The lowest is Idaho at $1,588. Debt is the Census Bureau total outstanding on Jun 30, 2024. Population is the Census Vintage 2025 estimate for Jul 1, 2025. Those dates are not the same day, so this is not debt per person on a single date. It is not a share of the federal debt.

What debt is counted?

Census total debt outstanding of the state government and of local governments. For the District of Columbia, Census publishes one government and that total is used. Unfunded pensions and the federal debt are not included.

Why do the debt date and the population date differ?

Census debt in this table is as of Jun 30, 2024. The population is the July 1 Vintage 2025 estimate, Jul 1, 2025. The page does not move either figure onto the other date.
