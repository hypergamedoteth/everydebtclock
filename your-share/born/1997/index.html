How your share is calculated

Your share = total public debt outstanding ÷ US resident population = $40,662,417,395,617.43 ÷ 341,784,857 = $118,970.80. The debt is Treasury’s Debt to the Penny close on Nov 16, 2026. The population is the Census Bureau’s 341,784,857 residents on Jul 1, 2025. Neither figure is projected. This is the official per-person figure on the national debt per person page, not the ticking clock.

The year you were born. The debt is Treasury’s Historical Debt Outstanding total at that fiscal year end (June 30 through FY1976, September 30 after that). Growth = the Nov 16, 2026 close ÷ that fiscal-year-end total. If the series has no row for the year, the page says so and does not fill one in.

Per person that year = the same fiscal-year-end total ÷ the Census July 1 resident population for that calendar year, and only when that July 1 count is already in the series used on the per-person page. The fiscal year end and July 1 are not the same day. A missing population is left missing.

A state = that state’s official debt total ÷ its Census resident population. The debt total is the sum of the official components on the state page (state and local, where the page has both), not the projected clock. It is that state’s own debt per resident. It is not a different share of the federal debt. The federal share is the same in every state.

Household size = the size you type × your share. Census’s own per-household figure is different: $40,662,417,395,617.43 ÷ 134,790,000 households = $301,672.36 (Census CPS ASEC 2025, Table HH-1, 2025).

Income = (the household total if you entered a size, otherwise your share) ÷ the annual income you typed. The income is not a published number.

It is a way to scale a very large number, not a bill. The population counts every resident, including children.
