Free debt clock widget

A free debt clock widget for a website. Paste one iframe. The clock shows the name, the live total, the per-second rate, and the source. Copy it into a blog post, a local news story, or a page from a political or civic group. The link under the frame points back to the full page. No account. The clock page sets no cookies and loads no analytics.

Add ?theme=light to the iframe address for a light background. The snippet is the iframe plus a plain link under it.

The countdown to $41 trillion is a separate widget at embed/41-trillion/. It follows the same rules: no cookies and no analytics. The snippet is on the $41 trillion page and the press page.
