    Questions

    How much is the US debt per person?

    US debt per person is $119,353.75 as of Nov 24, 2026, Treasury's total of $40,793,305,704,796.70 divided by 341,784,857 residents on Jul 1, 2025. Source: U.S. Treasury, Debt to the Penny, and the U.S. Census Bureau, Vintage 2025.

    What population is used?

    Debt per person uses the Census Vintage 2025 estimate of 341,784,857 residents on Jul 1, 2025, with no projection. Source: U.S. Census Bureau. A separate population clock on the homepage projects headcount forward. That projection is not the denominator.

    How much is the national debt per household?

    National debt per household is $302,643.41 as of Nov 24, 2026, Treasury's total divided by 134,790,000 households. Source: U.S. Treasury, Debt to the Penny, and Census CPS ASEC 2025.

    How much is the national debt per tax return?

    National debt per tax return filed is $250,642.70 as of Nov 24, 2026, Treasury's total divided by 162,754,810 individual returns in FY2025. Source: U.S. Treasury, Debt to the Penny, and IRS Data Book Table 1-2; a joint return counts once.

    Does the per-person figure use a projected population?

    No. The official $119,353.75 divides the Nov 24, 2026 Treasury close by 341,784,857 residents on Jul 1, 2025. The site does not divide the debt by a population projected past that Census date.
