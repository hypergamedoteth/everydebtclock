    US National Debt Clock

    The US national debt was $40,593,637,262,883.48 ($40.59 trillion) at the close of business on Oct 29, 2026, according to Treasury's Debt to the Penny. That is $118,769.56 per person: the debt divided by the Census Bureau's estimate of 341,784,857 US residents on Jul 1, 2025. The clock below starts from that official figure and projects forward at the 90-day trend (about $7.0 billion a day). It is re-based to Treasury's new figure every business day and never projects past the debt limit.
