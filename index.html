US National Debt Clock

The US national debt was $40,528,358,939,415.85 ($40.53 trillion) at the close of business on Oct 26, 2026, according to Treasury's Debt to the Penny. That is $118,578.57 per person: the debt divided by the Census Bureau's estimate of 341,784,857 US residents on Jul 1, 2025. The clock below starts from that official figure and projects forward at the 90-day trend (about $6.6 billion a day). It is re-based to Treasury's new figure every business day and never projects past the debt limit.
