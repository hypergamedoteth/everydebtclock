    US National Debt Clock

    The US national debt was $40,584,130,080,272.89 ($40.58 trillion) at Treasury's Nov 10, 2026 close. The figure is from Treasury's Debt to the Penny.

    That is $118,741.74 per person: the debt divided by the Census Bureau's estimate of 341,784,857 US residents on Jul 1, 2025. The clock below starts from that official figure and projects forward at the 90-day trend (about $7.2 billion a day). It is re-based to Treasury's new figure every business day and never projects past the debt limit.

    World Debt Clock. The headline for the United States is general government gross debt, $41,387,339,300,350 as of Dec 31, 2026 (IMF World Economic Outlook). This page's clock is narrower and is not in that total: federal debt, gross, Treasury Debt to the Penny, as of Nov 10, 2026.
