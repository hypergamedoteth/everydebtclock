    The debt limit

    The statutory debt limit is $41,103,996,000,000 ($41.104 trillion), per Treasury's Daily Treasury Statement. It applies to debt subject to the limit, which was $40,463,305,000,000 on Nov 16, 2026, leaving $640.7 billion of room. About $199.1 billion of the total debt is not subject to the limit, so total debt reaches $41 trillion when debt subject to the limit is about $40.801 trillion, still under the limit. The limit itself would bind with total debt near $41.30 trillion. At the 90-day pace of debt subject to the limit, the limit would be reached around February 12, 2027 (our projection). Once the limit binds, Treasury uses extraordinary measures and the debt goes roughly flat; our clock then stops projecting growth and holds at the official figure. This page counts toward $42 trillion only if Congress raises or suspends the limit before then.
