About Every Debt Clock

Run by Hypergame LLC, a Texas limited liability company.

Every Debt Clock is an independent site with live clocks for US federal debt, US state and local debt, and government debt in other countries. The US clock starts from Treasury's official close and ticks forward at the recent official pace. Official figures are labelled as official; anything projected is labelled as a projection.

Where the numbers come from

  • US federal debt. Treasury's Debt to the Penny. The live ticker starts from the latest official close and projects forward at the 90-day trend. It is re-based every business day and does not project past the statutory debt limit.
  • Interest, spending, revenue and the deficit. Treasury's Monthly Treasury Statement and Daily Treasury Statement, as labelled on each page.
  • Population. US Census Bureau estimates.
  • Other countries. Official national sources where we have them; otherwise IMF World Economic Outlook general government gross debt. Each country page names its source.
  • US states and Washington, DC. Official Census figures for state and local debt. Each state page names its source.

The full method, including how the ticker is checked against the next Treasury close, is on the methodology page. Downloadable copies of the series are on the data page.

How often it updates

US federal figures are rebuilt after Treasury publishes a new Debt to the Penny close, on business days. State, country and IMF pages are updated when those sources publish.
