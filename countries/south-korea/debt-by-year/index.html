South Korea national debt by year

Answer. South Korea's general government gross debt was $1,062,087,157,905 ($1.06 trillion) as of Dec 31, 2026, up $444.08 billion (+71.9%) from $618.00 billion on Dec 31, 2016. The 2026 figure is the IMF projection for the end of 2026, not a closed national ledger. Source: IMF World Economic Outlook, October 2026, GGXWDG_NGDP, general government gross debt, percent of GDP.
