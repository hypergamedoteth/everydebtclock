    Country Debt Clocks: National Debt by Country

    Every place with a debt clock, built from figures its own government publishes. A clock ticks only where the official trend is reliable; otherwise the official figure is shown as published. Each clock shows who publishes the numbers and how often, with every number linked to its source. Most of these governments report their debt once a year or once a month, so the ticking numbers are labelled projections from the latest official figures. Each tile says what it counts: state clocks count state and local government debt (cities, counties, school and special districts included), and countries are shown in their own currency. What this list counts: the first section is each country's own published figure, in its own currency. Harmonized rows are Eurostat Maastricht general-government debt or a World Bank/IMF QPSD general-government stock. The IMF section is the World Economic Outlook estimate where no general-government stock is on this site. The World Debt Clock adds general government gross debt once per economy. A narrower series on a country page is not added again.
