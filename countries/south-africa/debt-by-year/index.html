South Africa national debt by year

Answer. South Africa's general government gross debt was $381,699,471,468 ($381.70 billion) as of Dec 31, 2026, up $229.22 billion (+150.3%) from $152.48 billion on Dec 31, 2016. The 2026 figure is the IMF projection for the end of 2026, not a closed national ledger. Source: IMF World Economic Outlook, October 2026, GGXWDG_NGDP, general government gross debt, percent of GDP.
