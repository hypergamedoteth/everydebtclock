    Canada National Debt Clock

    The Government of Canada owed C$1,665,761,000,000 (C$1.67 trillion) on Oct 31, 2026, according to the Bank of Canada, Government of Canada direct securities and loans: Distribution of holdings. This is the Government of Canada's own borrowing in securities, the closest Canadian match to the US national debt clock's Total Public Debt Outstanding: everything it has issued and not yet repaid, including securities held by the Bank of Canada. Provincial and municipal debt is not included. Amounts are in Canadian dollars. That is C$39,852.26 per Canadian: the debt divided by Statistics Canada's estimate of 41,798,407 Canada residents on Jul 1, 2026. The clock below starts from that official figure and projects forward at the past 12 months' pace (about C$307.3M a day), the official change from Oct 31, 2025 to Oct 31, 2026. Canada reports its debt once a month, not daily, so the ticking number is a projection, not an official figure; it re-bases when the next monthly figures come out.

    Source tier: official national series. As of Oct 31, 2026. Coverage: Federal government securities outstanding, gross.

    World Debt Clock. The headline for Canada is general government gross debt, $2,878,018,598,854 as of Dec 31, 2026 (IMF World Economic Outlook, October 2026).     How it works

    Official monthly figures · projection labelled · sources linked

    The live number = latest official total + (the official change per second over the past 12 months × seconds since that total). Canada publishes its debt once a month, not daily, so this is a projection between monthly figures, not an official figure.

    projected = 1,665,761,000,000 + 3,556.6971 × (seconds since Oct 31, 2026 11:59:59 PM CT)

    The rate is the official change from Oct 31, 2025 to Oct 31, 2026: C$1,665,761,000,000 − C$1,553,597,000,000 = C$112,164,000,000 over 365 days = C$3,556.70 per second. We use 12 months rather than the last month because month-to-month changes swing by tens of billions with bond maturities and treasury-bill rollovers. The actual path is lumpy (bonds are sold in batches and repaid on schedules), so the projection will drift; when new monthly figures come out, the clock re-bases to the new official figures.

    How often it's published. Monthly. The Bank of Canada publishes each month-end figure in the first days of the following month (series V42141673). The November 2026 month-end figure is expected in early December 2026; this page re-bases to it automatically on the next daily build after it appears.

    Per Canadian divides by Statistics Canada's estimate of 41,798,407 residents on Jul 1, 2026. It is not projected forward, so the live per-Canadian figure rises only with the debt.

    Note. Why this measure: the US clock ticks Treasury's Total Public Debt Outstanding, the face value of all marketable and non-marketable Treasury securities including those held by the Federal Reserve and by government trust funds, with inflation compensation on TIPS. The Bank of Canada's total securities outstanding (inflation adjusted) is the Canadian series built the same way and published monthly.

    Note. Canada's federal debt (accumulated deficit) is a different measure: liabilities minus assets, including pensions and accounts payable. It is shown as a separate figure, not added to the clock.

    Note. Not included: provincial, territorial and municipal debt; Crown corporation debt; pension and other liabilities. The US-dollar figure converts the official month-end total at the Bank of Canada's latest daily rate and is for comparison only.

    Note. Figures from source tables may not add exactly because of rounding in the source.
