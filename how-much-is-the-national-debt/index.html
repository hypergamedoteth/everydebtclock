$40,709,780,700,317

Answer. The US national debt (total public debt outstanding) was $40,709,780,700,317.02, or about $40.71 trillion, at the close of business on Nov 19, 2026, the latest figure Treasury has published. That is how much debt the US federal government is in: $119,109.37 for each of the 341,784,857 people the Census Bureau counted on Jul 1, 2025.
