How much is the national debt?

Treasury, Debt to the Penny · close of business Oct 23, 2026

$40,463,151,897,249

Answer. The US national debt (total public debt outstanding) was $40,463,151,897,249.19, or about $40.46 trillion, at the close of business on Oct 23, 2026, the latest figure Treasury has published. That is how much debt the US federal government is in: $118,387.78 for each of the 341,784,857 people the Census Bureau counted on Jul 1, 2025.
