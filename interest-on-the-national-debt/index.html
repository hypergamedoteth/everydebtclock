    Interest on the national debt

    Cash interest paid on Treasury securities was $45,130,000,000 through Nov 12, 2026, the Daily Treasury Statement close. The figure above is a projection from that close and has moved since then. Payments land in lumps, so the digits jump on coupon dates. On an accrual basis, Treasury's interest-expense dataset is running at about $3.8 billion a day, the last 12 reported months spread over the year. Net interest, the budget total, was $1,051,753,969,311 in FY2026. MTS net interest for FY2027 through Oct 31, 2026 is $103,076,056,306. That is a year-to-date total, not a finished year.

    By fiscal year

    Net interest is MTS Table 9, line 320, the September year-to-date total. Interest expense is the sum of every line in Treasury's Interest Expense on the Public Debt Outstanding dataset for that September. The dataset total is larger. It counts interest on all Treasury securities, including securities held by government accounts. Net interest is the budget figure after interest the government receives.
