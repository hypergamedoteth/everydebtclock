    Questions

    How much interest has the US paid this fiscal year?

    $132,511,000,000 in cash interest on Treasury securities as of Nov 19, 2026, from the Daily Treasury Statement. The live estimate above that figure is a projection and has moved since that close.

    What is net interest on the national debt?

    Net interest was $1,051,753,969,311 in FY2026 (Monthly Treasury Statement, line 320). It is the budget total, smaller than the interest-expense dataset because interest received by the government is netted out.

    How much interest does the US pay per day?

    Treasury's interest-expense dataset totaled $1,371,454,499,553 in the 12 months ending Oct 31, 2026, about $3.8 billion a day on an accrual basis. Source: U.S. Treasury, Interest Expense on the Public Debt Outstanding; cash interest is paid in lumps, and the live clock instead multiplies the debt by Treasury's average rate and divides by 365.25.

    Is the live estimate the official interest total?

    No. The live estimate above the official close is projected forward from the Daily Treasury Statement close of $132,511,000,000 as of Nov 19, 2026. Payments land on coupon dates, so the official cash total stays flat between those dates and then jumps.

    Where do the interest figures come from?

    Cash interest this fiscal year is the Daily Treasury Statement. Net interest by fiscal year is Monthly Treasury Statement Table 9, line 320. Interest expense by fiscal year is Treasury's Interest Expense on the Public Debt Outstanding dataset. They measure different things.
